# About

Healthcare today is fragmented.

Medical records are scattered across hospitals, laboratories, pharmacies, clinics, and diagnostic centers, making continuity of care difficult for both patients and healthcare professionals.

**NuroVed** is designed to solve this challenge by creating a connected digital healthcare ecosystem where patients remain at the center of every healthcare interaction.

This repository contains the official landing page showcasing the vision, ecosystem, AI capabilities, and healthcare platform developed under the **Promacle** vision.
