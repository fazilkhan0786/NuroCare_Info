1) Information we collect

  • Account & profile data: name and basic details you provide (e.g., patient or professional role).

2) How we use information

  • Provide platform features (bookings, digital reports, e-prescriptions, and role-based dashboards).

  • Improve user experience, reliability, and security.
  • Communicate with you about demos or platform-related requests.

3) Data sharing inside the ecosystem

NuroCare is designed to connect stakeholders. When you use features that involve other roles (for example, a Lab uploading a report or a Pharmacy receiving an e-prescription), relevant data may be shared with those parties as part of delivering that service. Access is intended to be role-based and purpose-limited.

4) Government & public health use

The Government dashboard includes epidemic heatmaps and resource insights. When such features are used, data may be aggregated to help officials understand trends such as outbreaks or hospital bed demand. Where possible, aggregation is preferred over identifying individuals.

5) Security

We aim to use secure system design and controlled access to protect data. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security. You should only upload and share information you are comfortable providing digitally.

6) AI features and limitations

AI outputs are informational support tools and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For urgent symptoms or emergencies, contact local emergency services immediately.
